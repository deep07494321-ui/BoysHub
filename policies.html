Frequently Asked Questions

How do I place an order?

Choose your products, add them to your cart, enter your delivery details and submit the order.

Which payment methods are available?

Cash on Delivery (COD) and a UPI QR option may be shown at checkout. UPI payments are not automatically verified by this demo website; confirm payment with your bank/UPI app before treating an order as paid.

How can I check my order status?

Contact customer support with your order number. Automatic order tracking is not available until the order database and shipping provider are connected.

Shipping & Delivery

Delivery availability and estimated delivery time depend on the destination PIN code, courier service and order processing. Shipping charges, delivery estimates and tracking details should be confirmed at checkout or by customer support.

Shiprocket integration is not active yet. Shipment booking, AWB generation, courier tracking and COD remittance are not currently automated on this website.

Returns, Exchanges & Refunds

Please contact customer support before returning an item and provide your order number, product details and reason for the request. Eligibility, return window, product condition requirements, pickup arrangements, exchanges and refund timelines must be confirmed by the store before the return is approved.

Do not send an item back until support has provided return instructions. Refunds for UPI or other prepaid orders should only be considered after payment is verified.

Privacy Policy

Checkout may ask for your name, mobile number, delivery address, PIN code and order details to process a purchase and assist with delivery. Share only information needed for your order. This current demo stores order details in the browser on the device used to place the order; it does not yet provide a secure, shared order database or automatic SMS notifications.

Before launching to the public, the store owner should add a production privacy policy explaining data storage, retention, access, service providers, customer rights and how to request deletion or correction.

Terms & Conditions

Orders may require confirmation of stock, delivery availability and payment status. Customers should contact support to resolve order or product issues.
